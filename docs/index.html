What it does

The drone flies from A to B or holds a position, without GPS, in one of the hardest everyday indoor spaces: narrow aisles, hanging leaves, thin wires, heating pipes, glass walls and people pushing trolleys.

SenseForward depth camera with an object detector, plus small time-of-flight distance sensors around the body.
RememberA local map of what it has seen, and a route through it.
ThinkA neural network trained with reinforcement learning proposes the next command, 30 times a second.
CheckA rule-based safety layer checks every command so the drone can always stop in time. PX4 flies the safe command.

How it was tested

Each step is harder and more independent than the one before.

01
Simulator exam

1,000 fixed greenhouse tasks, the same for every version, so versions can be compared flight by flight.

02
Gazebo + PX4

Different physics, different sensor rendering and real flight software. The brain was never trained there.

03
Broken sensors

Position drift, heading errors, noise, mix-ups, dead sensors and extra delay, on purpose.

04
A real drone

Next: run the brain on board, first in an empty hall, then in a greenhouse.

Honest limits

  • Simulation only, so far. Real sensors, real air and real localization will bring new problems.
  • In the simulator exam about 1 % of flights still crash, mostly a person approaching from behind or the side, or an obstacle that appears suddenly in front.
  • With poor localization the drone gets slow in narrow spots. It stays safe, but may not finish in time.
  • The replays above are drawn from the logged positions; the plants and people are the simulated ones from the same world.
